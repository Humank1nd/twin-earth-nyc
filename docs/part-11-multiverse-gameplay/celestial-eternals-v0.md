## 3. The Emergence Event (High-Heat)

Emergence occurs when planetary Energy ($E$) reaches a critical threshold ($T$).

### 3.1 Energy Equation
$$E = P \times C_{life}$$
- $P$: Total intelligent population.
- $C_{life}$: Constant for energy conversion per sapient mind.

If $E \ge T$, an Emergence event is triggered, causing:
1. **Physical Heat:** +0.8 (Seismic spikes, infrastructure stress).
2. **Ecological Heat:** +0.5 (Reality shimmer, background radiation).
3. **Institutional Heat:** +0.3 (SHIELD/Authority focus).

## 4. Translation Gate Constraints

Celestial artifacts (e.g., Eternal technology) are decayed by the **Bifrost Bandwidth Cap** (Phase 39) when entering the Hub.

- **Uni-Mind Decay:** Pooling efficiency reduced by 70% in Hub.
- **Power Decay:** Transmutation/Energy projection reduced to "advanced props" status.
